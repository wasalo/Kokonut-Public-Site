**Kokonut Network fully embodies the spirit of a “DAO.”**

To realize our vision, we are deploying multiple instances of the same core thesis: Community-Governed Decentralized Autonomous Entities.

We recognize that no single solution can solve the complex challenges of human coordination. That’s why we’re committed to embracing a variety of governance mechanisms, frameworks, and platforms.

Our goal is to build a resilient hyperstructure that unites diverse stakeholders, without relying on a single token or point of failure.

### Deployed Instances

| **Name** | **Purpose** |
| --- | --- |
| [Kokonut Moloch DAO](/ecosystem-wiki/the-kokonut-dao/kokonut-moloch-dao) | Kokonut Moloch DAO exists to expand and scale the ecosystem by funding, overseeing, and governing projects approved by the DAO. |
| [Kokonut Guilds DAO](/ecosystem-wiki/the-kokonut-dao/kokonut-guilds-dao) | Kokonut Guilds are our approach to meta-governance. They feature fluid, merit-based membership, where contributions earn points that function as reputation within the organization. This reputation powers merit-based governance across our operational layer. |
