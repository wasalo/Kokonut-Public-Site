# Kokonut is open coordination infrastructure for regenerative agriculture.

Kokonut Network connects **locally operated regenerative farms** with capital governance, contributor coordination, shared implementation standards, public evidence, and open-source intelligence.

[Adelphi](/ecosystem-wiki/kokonut-farms/adelphi/summary), in Monte Plata, Dominican Republic, is the first live reference implementation. It gives the ecosystem a real place to test governance, farm-development methods, data structures, MRV, and community participation before those ideas are replicated elsewhere.

## Kokonut in one minute

- **Farm first:** Adelphi is a live syntropic farm with public records, operators, infrastructure, crops, poultry, and active MRV workflows.
- **Different responsibilities use different coordination systems:** the Moloch DAO governs capital and treasury decisions; Guilds coordinate contribution; local operators run the farm; the Framework provides shared methodology.
- **Evidence matters:** MRV connects field activity, remote sensing, farm records, and attestations to public reporting through the Kokonut Hub and related infrastructure.
- **The Framework is reusable, not rigid:** new farms can share data structures, development phases, evidence standards, and coordination interfaces while adapting to local ecology, culture, operators, markets, and regulation.
- **Participation is not limited to capital:** builders, researchers, agronomists, organizers, and other contributors can participate through useful work and Guild pathways.
- **The technical layer is expanding:** Kokonut Intelligence is active infrastructure, while more advanced AI-agent automation remains in development.

## How Kokonut fits together

Kokonut treats regenerative agriculture as a coordination problem with several distinct responsibilities.

The ecosystem does **not** depend on one DAO making every decision.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    PEOPLE["Community + Contributors"]

    subgraph COORDINATION["Coordination Systems"]
        CAPITAL["Moloch DAO<br/>Capital + Treasury"]
        GUILDS["Guilds<br/>Contribution + Domains"]
        FRAMEWORK["Kokonut Framework<br/>Shared Methodology"]
    end

    FARM["Local Farm Operators<br/>Real-world execution"]
    MRV["MRV<br/>Evidence + Records"]
    HUB["Kokonut Hub<br/>Public Data"]
    KI["Kokonut Intelligence<br/>Analysis + Automation"]

    PEOPLE --> CAPITAL
    PEOPLE --> GUILDS

    CAPITAL --> FARM
    GUILDS --> FRAMEWORK
    FRAMEWORK --> FARM

    FARM --> MRV
    MRV --> HUB
    MRV --> KI

    KI --> CAPITAL
    KI --> GUILDS
    KI --> FARM
```

| Layer | Primary role |
| --- | --- |
| **Moloch DAO** | Capital membership, treasury governance, proposals, voting, execution, and rage-quit protection |
| **Guilds** | Contribution coordination, domain work, reputation, bounties, and contributor pathways |
| **Kokonut Framework** | Shared farm-development methodology, data structures, development phases, and impact logic |
| **Local farm operators** | Day-to-day agricultural decisions and real-world execution |
| **MRV** | Measurement, reporting, verification, and evidence quality |
| **Kokonut Hub** | Public-facing farm and project records |
| **Kokonut Intelligence** | Data integration, analytics, synthesis, reporting, automation, and decision support |
| **AI-agent infrastructure** | Developing automation for repeatable analytical and coordination tasks |

> **The goal is interoperability, not uniformity.** Farms can share a common baseline without pretending every landscape, community, operator, market, or regulatory environment is the same.

## One ecosystem, different coordination systems

Kokonut separates responsibilities because capital, contribution, farm operations, evidence, and technical infrastructure require different forms of accountability.

## What is live and what is still developing

Transparency about maturity matters.

| Component | Status | What that means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Adelphi Farm** | **Live** | Active reference farm in Monte Plata with public records and ongoing operations |
| **Kokonut Moloch DAO** | **Live** | Governance, treasury, membership, token, proposal, and rage-quit infrastructure deployed on Gnosis Chain |
| **Kokonut Framework** | **Live and evolving** | Public methodology already applied to Adelphi and continuously improved |
| **MRV pipeline** | **Active and expanding** | Field, harvest, remote-sensing, and impact records support public verification; coverage and automation continue to improve |
| **Kokonut Hub** | **Live** | Public-facing project and farm-data surface for Adelphi |
| **Kokonut Intelligence** | **Active infrastructure** | Data, analytics, research, synthesis, scoring, and reporting infrastructure |
| **AI-agent runtime and automation** | **Developing** | Task-specific automation for MRV, forecasting, reporting, scoring, identity, and payments is still being built |
| **Multi-farm replication** | **Next frontier** | The next major test is applying the shared model to additional farms without rebuilding the entire coordination stack |

<Note>
  A documented concept is not the same as a deployed system. Kokonut documentation should keep those maturity levels explicit as the ecosystem evolves.

## The Kokonut coordination loop

The core loop is designed to move **capital, work, evidence, and learning** through different parts of the ecosystem.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A["Capital governance<br/>approves funding"] --> B["Local farm operators<br/>execute"]
    B --> C["MRV records<br/>farm activity"]
    C --> D["Hub + Intelligence<br/>structure evidence"]
    D --> E["Governance + contributors<br/>review what happened"]
    E --> F["Framework + Guilds<br/>improve methods and tools"]
    F --> G["Next farm or next cycle<br/>starts with better infrastructure"]
    G --> B
```

This is different from promising that one token, treasury, or automation system solves every problem.

The intended feedback loop is:

1. Capital decisions are made through the appropriate governance process.
2. Farm operators execute in the real world.
3. Farm activity becomes structured evidence.
4. Evidence informs reporting, governance, contributor work, and operational decisions.
5. The Framework, tools, and coordination processes improve.
6. Future farm cycles begin with more reusable knowledge and infrastructure.

Where farm revenue is involved, allocation depends on the approved economic and governance arrangements for that farm. Revenue forecasts are not guaranteed returns.

## Why coconuts?

Coconut trees are one of Kokonut's long-cycle anchors inside diversified syntropic systems.

They matter for several reasons:

- **Long productive life:** coconut is a perennial crop rather than a single-season harvest.
- **Multiple potential product streams:** water, food, oil, fiber, husk, compost inputs, and other processed products can create different market pathways.
- **Syntropic compatibility:** coconut trees can be integrated into multi-strata, biodiverse production systems rather than isolated monocultures.
- **Governance relationship:** Kokonut's current \$vKKN model uses a 1:1 relationship between governance tokens and coconut trees.
- **Renewal principle:** the model is designed around replanting when trees reach the end of productive life rather than treating the underlying natural asset as disposable.

Coconut is not the entire farm model. Adelphi combines long-cycle trees with shorter-cycle crops, passion fruit, poultry, biodiversity, soil regeneration, infrastructure, and community activity.

Understanding the boundaries is as important as understanding the ambition.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Not one DAO for everything" icon="diagram-project">

  <Card title="Not a one-size-fits-all farm template" icon="leaf">
