## What we're building

Grassroots farmers control less than 10% of the global coconut market — not because their land is unproductive, but because they lack access to capital, coordination tools, and transparent governance structures.

Kokonut Network is a **blockchain-based cooperative** that closes this gap. We partner with farmers, raise funds through a DAO, deploy syntropic farms, and distribute rewards perpetually to everyone in the network. Blockchain handles the trust layer — transparent treasury, soulbound tokens, on-chain governance, and rage-quit protections. The land handles the rest.

Our model has no expiration date. When a coconut tree ends its productive life, we replant it. The cooperative continues. The community owns the upside.

## How the model works

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Fund" icon="coins">
    DAO members tribute stablecoins to the treasury. Every token minted is backed 1:1 by a real coconut tree. No admin can override. Rage-quit protects every member.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Grow" icon="seedling">
    Approved farms receive funding and deploy the Kokonut Framework: syntropic methods, satellite MRV, on-chain attestations, and community training — all standardized.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Distribute" icon="arrows-split-up-and-left">
    Revenue flows back to token holders, contributors, and the communities running the farms. The cycle restarts when trees are replanted. No end date.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

> **The Kokonut Framework is the repeatable piece.** Every farm runs the same playbook — common data schema, four development phases, eight forms of capital measured. New farms slot in without reinventing the model. [Read the Framework →](/kokonut-framework/Introduction)
