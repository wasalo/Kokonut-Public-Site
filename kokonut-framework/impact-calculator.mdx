**How every number is computed:**

Carbon sequestration pulls directly from the MRV docs — 0.4–1.2 metric tons CO₂e per acre per year for regenerative agriculture, converted to m². Crop mix drives the rate: short-cycle vegetables alone sit at the 0.4 baseline; adding medium-cycle fruit raises it to 0.7; the full short\+medium\+long agroforestry mix hits 1.2. Biochar adds an 18% boost, which is conservative compared to the literature.

Revenue uses per-m² averages derived from the Adelphi harvest forecast data: lettuce at ~\$26.65/m²/yr, passion fruit at ~\$2.20/m², coconut at ~\$0.97/m². The full-mix figure blends these. The 10% public goods line is directly from the Adelphi schema.
