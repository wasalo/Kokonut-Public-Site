Kokonut Framework Tool

Estimate how a regenerative farm maps to SDG impact.

Use this calculator to model how farm size, labor, crop diversity, certification, biodiversity work, and MRV readiness can influence indicative SDG contribution scores.

Use as a planning tool, not a certified assessment. Scores are indicative. Impact claims should be verified through farm records, MRV payloads, public Data Hub entries, and annual reports before they are used in proposals or public reporting.

Assumptions

InputHow it affects the model
Crop systemMore diverse systems increase food, biodiversity, and resilience scores, but can reduce simple revenue-per-m² assumptions.
WorkersEmployment affects SDG 1 and SDG 8, especially when paired with training and stable farm operations.
Women-ledWomen-led ownership or management increases SDG 5 and reinforces household and community resilience.
MRV-readyMRV readiness improves confidence because claims can be checked against records, attestations, and reports.
