# Source library

This is the detailed reference beneath the visual-first [As Seen On](/traction/as-seen-on) page. It preserves **all 30 source entries** submitted by Kokonut, including both URLs for the two items that provide alternate media formats. The collection is historical, and this is a snapshot of the supplied URLs—not a live link-health dashboard.

## How to interpret the entries

- **Verified** means the publisher's page, accessible source text, or a credible indexed extract explicitly supports the described Kokonut connection.
- **Source supplied** means the URL comes from Kokonut's collection but the media or full article was not independently retrievable or playable during this review. We do not invent the missing details.
- **Context only** means the resource is thematically connected to Web3, ReFi, or DAOs, but an explicit Kokonut mention was not found in the accessible material. It is **not** presented as a press feature.
- **Multi-format** sources are **one appearance with two links**, not two independent press features.

This page is an editorial research and provenance inventory, **not an assertion of third-party endorsement**, current partner status, audited impact, or live deployment. Older reports and interviews reflect Kokonut's position at the time of publication.

## A source-quality note on embeds

The primary page provides YouTube and Spotify iframes based on the media IDs in the submitted URLs, with direct external links alongside them. Availability, autoplay restrictions, geography, account requirements, or author permissions may affect playback. The supplied X Space is linked directly because its replay and reliable embeddability could not be confirmed. The source itself remains the authority for what is published.

## Curating future coverage

When adding entries, record the title, publisher, original source, publication date if verified, exact subject of the Kokonut mention, whether it is **direct coverage**, a **curated mention**, a **collaboration**, or **background context**, the supported playback format, and the relationship between duplicate audio/video versions. Keep any screenshots, quotes, and performance claims separately evidence-backed.
